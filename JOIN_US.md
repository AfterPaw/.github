# 🐾 Join the AfterPaw Team

We are expanding our indie studio and actively looking for talented, passionate creators to collaborate on upcoming original titles for **Steam**!

If you are passionate about independent game development, storytelling, puzzle mechanics, and LGBTQ+/furry themes, we would love to hear from you.

---

## 🎯 Open Roles

### 🎨 2D Artists & Illustrators
We are looking for creative artists with strong illustration and visual storytelling skills.

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Character design, concept art, and visual development.
  - Visual novel character sprites (expressions, poses, outfits) and CG illustrations.
  - Background art, UI elements, and promotional art assets.
  - (Optional / Project-dependent) Mature / NSFW art for adult-themed projects.
- **Experience / Tools:**
  - Digital illustration software (Clip Studio Paint, Photoshop, Procreate, or equivalent).
  - Experience with character consistency and game-ready asset preparation.
  - Familiarity with furry art culture and expressive styles is a huge plus.

---

### 🧊 3D Modelers & Texture Artists
We are seeking 3D artists capable of bringing unique characters and game environments to life.

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Creating stylized 3D character models, props, and environment assets.
  - UV unwrapping, texturing, and basic rigging/skinning.
  - Optimizing 3D assets for real-time game engines.
- **Experience / Tools:**
  - Blender, Maya, ZBrush, Substance Painter, or equivalent tools.
  - Experience exporting assets for game engines (Unity / Godot / Unreal).
  - Portfolio showcasing low-to-mid poly models and clean topology.

---

### 💻 Game Programmers & Developers
We are looking for developers to build clean gameplay systems, UI, and custom mechanics.

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Implementing core gameplay mechanics (puzzle systems, logic, interaction).
  - Integrating narrative dialogue systems, branching paths, and save/load systems.
  - UI/UX implementation and platform features (Steamworks integration, achievements, controller support).
- **Experience / Tools:**
  - Game Engines: Unity (C#), Godot (GDScript / C#), or Ren'Py / custom frameworks.
  - Experience with Git version control and collaborative workflows.
  - Problem-solving mindset with attention to game feel and polish.
